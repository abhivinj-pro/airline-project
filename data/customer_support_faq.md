# AirWave Customer Support FAQ

## When is my booking confirmed?
Your booking is confirmed only after the traveler completes the payment step and clicks Pay & Confirm Booking. AirAssist should not say that a booking is confirmed before that happens.

## When is the confirmation email sent?
The confirmation email is sent only after a booking is successfully completed. If payment has not been completed, there is no confirmation email yet.

## Which payment methods are supported?
Travelers can complete the booking with a standard card payment on the payment step. Visa, Mastercard, and Amex are the supported example methods.

## Can I cancel for free?
Yes. AirWave offers free cancellation within 24 hours of booking completion.

## Is baggage included?
The standard economy fares in this demo include one cabin bag. Checked baggage depends on the selected fare and any extras added during the booking flow.

## Does price lock cost extra?
In this demo, when the booking flow shows a price lock, it holds the current fare for 4 hours. No separate price-lock fee is charged unless a fee is explicitly shown in the booking flow.

## Can I add extras later?
Yes. Extras like baggage, seat selection, lounge access, meals, and insurance can be added during the booking flow, and post-booking servicing may be offered separately.

## Can I change the passenger name after booking?
Name corrections and changes require support review after booking. The traveler should contact support instead of assuming the name can be edited automatically.

## When should I check in?
Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure for most AirWave flights.

## What if I need help on the payment page?
AirAssist can explain the payment step and fare rules. For large international group bookings at the payment step, AirAssist may offer a human specialist handoff.
